# Friction log

One line per session, written by `debrief`, so the skills change on counts instead of impressions.

Line format:

```
<YYYY-MM-DD> · <lesson + step> · <N loops on what> · <what unlocked it> · pace: ok / too hard
```

A loop is one more explanation after a miss on the same idea.

## Change on trial

*None. At most one change at a time: one edit to one skill file, agreed by the learner, with its start date, the
metric expected to move, and the replaced text. Judged after three sessions against the three before.*
