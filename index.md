# Index

Entry point to the vault.

## Start

Fill in [[LEARNER]] once. Then say one of these to Claude Code, run from this folder:

- "I want to learn ..." or "my goal is ..." breaks a goal, a track or a topic into strands and writes a track page (`teacher`).
- "teach me ..." starts a lesson on one strand (`teacher`).
- "continue ..." resumes a lesson at its `next` step (`teacher`).
- "practice" is optional: it retests what you choose, or what the queue suggests (`practice`).
- "debrief" ends a lesson or practice session and logs it (`debrief`).

## Where things are

- [[LEARNER]]: who you are and how you learn.
- [[levels]]: your level and current edge per strand.
- [[friction-log]]: one line per session, and the change on trial.
- `tracks/`: track pages, for example [[example-track]].
- `subjects/`: lesson notes, grouped by subject.
- `practice/`: practice notes.
